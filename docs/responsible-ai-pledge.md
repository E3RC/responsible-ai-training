# E3RC Responsible AI Commitment

As a member of E3 Robotics Center and FIRST Robotics Competition Team 1555, I will use artificial intelligence to expand my abilities—not replace my responsibility.

I will:

- verify important information before I rely on it;
- protect private, sensitive, confidential, and credential information;
- follow the AI-use rules set by my teachers, mentors, team, and organizations;
- disclose meaningful AI assistance when appropriate;
- give people proper credit for their work and ideas;
- avoid using AI to deceive, impersonate, bully, harass, exploit, or harm others;
- review and test AI-generated technical work before it is used on a robot or other system;
- respect safety requirements and stop when AI-generated advice conflicts with official documentation or human judgment;
- remember that AI can reflect bias, make mistakes, invent facts, and sound confident when it is wrong;
- remain accountable for work I submit, publish, build, deploy, or recommend.

I understand that responsible AI use is consistent with the spirit of Discovery, Innovation, Impact, Inclusion, Teamwork, Gracious Professionalism, Coopertition, and safe, high-quality engineering.

This commitment is part of the E3RC Responsible Artificial Intelligence Certification and is not an official FIRST certification.
