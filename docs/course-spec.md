# E3RC Responsible AI Certification — Course Specification

## Audience

High-school students participating through E3 Robotics Center, with primary use by FIRST Robotics Competition Team 1555.

## Course purpose

Teach students to use artificial intelligence responsibly, safely, honestly, and effectively in three settings:

1. Team 1555 / E3RC robotics work
2. Regular schoolwork
3. Personal and online life

The course should encourage capable use of AI rather than treating AI itself as misconduct. The governing principle is that students may use AI to extend their abilities, but may not outsource responsibility.

## Learning framework

Students should repeatedly encounter five rules:

1. **VERIFY** — AI output is not automatically true.
2. **PROTECT** — Do not give AI systems private, sensitive, credential, or confidential information.
3. **DISCLOSE** — Be honest about AI assistance when rules, context, or authorship make disclosure appropriate.
4. **RESPECT** — Do not use AI to deceive, bully, impersonate, exploit, or harm others.
5. **OWN IT** — The human user remains responsible for work submitted, published, built, or deployed.

## FIRST alignment

The curriculum should connect responsible AI use to FIRST culture and values, especially:

- Discovery
- Innovation
- Impact
- Inclusion
- Teamwork
- Fun
- Gracious Professionalism
- Coopertition
- Safety and high-quality work

The training is an E3 Robotics Center program and must not be represented as an official FIRST certification.

## Recommended duration

Target completion time: 2 to 2.5 hours.

## Modules

1. AI Is a Tool, Not an Authority
2. Verify Before You Trust
3. AI and Schoolwork
4. AI in Engineering and Robotics
5. Teamwork, Credit, and Authorship
6. Bias, Fairness, and Inclusion
7. Privacy, Security, and Confidential Information
8. Deepfakes, Misinformation, and Personal Life
9. Competition, Coopertition, and Community Responsibility
10. Responsible AI Commitment and Final Assessment

## Completion model

- Students must complete each module.
- Each module should include reading and/or video content.
- Each module should include 3–7 knowledge or scenario questions.
- Explanations should be shown after questions so the assessment teaches as well as measures.
- Module checks may be retried.
- Final assessment should draw 20 randomized questions from a larger bank.
- Recommended passing score: 80%.
- Students who do not pass should receive topic-level remediation guidance before retrying.

## Pre/post measurement

Use a short ungraded pre-course check and repeat selected concepts at the end. Store aggregate improvement metrics where practical so E3RC can demonstrate training impact.

## Certificate

Credential name:

**E3RC Responsible Artificial Intelligence Certification**

Subtitle:

**FIRST Robotics Competition — Team 1555**

Suggested descriptor:

**Responsible AI Use • Engineering • Academic Integrity • Digital Citizenship**

Certificates should include:

- Student name
- E3 Robotics Center
- FRC Team 1555
- Completion date
- Final score
- Curriculum version
- Unique certificate ID
- QR code linking to verification

## Privacy

Collect only what is needed for certification. Recommended fields:

- Student name
- Team number (fixed: 1555)
- Organization (fixed: E3 Robotics Center)
- Completion date
- Score
- Curriculum version
- Certificate ID
- Email address only when the student explicitly requests email delivery

Do not collect birth date, home address, phone number, or unrelated student records.
